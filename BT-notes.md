## High Warlord Naj'entus
- !! Impaling Spine: targets random player (but not the main tank); deals heavy damage, stuns the player, has a DoT; (4.5k dmg, 2.7 dmg every 3 seconds until removed)
    - another player has to move to the player and pick up the spine to use during Tidal Shield
- !! Tidal Shield: the boss becomes immune to all attacks, and heals 1% every 2s
    - removed by clicking the spine in that person's inventory
    - this deals 8.5k frost damag to raid, so everyone needs to be topped up
    - casted every ~60s
- Needle Spine: ~3k damage, ~2k to nearby players within 6 yards -> soft spread
    - targets 3 random players

- lots of damage going out, players should seek to have 10k hp
    - healthstones, ideally two; healing pots
- tldr: Impaling Spine and Tidal Shield
- light spread

## Supremus
- two phases, swaps about every minute
- phase 1: 
    - hateful strike: looks at the 3 highest-threat melee, and casts it on the one that has the highest health, but not the main tank. Deals 20k-27k dmg; have this be the off-tank
    - molten flame: ground aoe that looks like blue flame; spawns at boss's feet and creates a trail, following a random target
        - will chase the target for 10s, then lingers on the ground for another 10s
- phase 2:
    - boss becomes empowered, dealing more damage but moving slower
    - boss will fixate on a player and moves towards them; will 1-shot if he hits
        - before fixating, will cast Charge, which kicks the fixated player away, so its safe for melee to continue to deal damage 
    - Volcanic Geysers spawn randomly on the ground which cause fire damage to nearby players, avoid these; despawns after ~20s; stay 15+ yards away
- !! threat drop at phase shift from 2 -> 1
    - misdirects

- OT needed for hateful strikes, needs to be 2nd/3rd in threat, not incl. ranged
    - OT needs to be topped off quickly, otherwise the hateful strike will hit a melee dps
- spread for phase 2, boss will change fixate targets quickly
- threat drop from phase 2->1, dps should stop

## Shade of Akama
- phase 1:
    - burn down the 6 channelers
    - adds will spawn in groups of 3, left and right sides of the room (by doorway)
        - prio Ashtongue Spiritbinder, CC elementalists and rogues
- phase 2:
    - dps race to kill the shade before the shade kills akama
    - akama tanks the shade; tanks tank remaining adds

- misc: after kill this boss, a teleport unlocks from entrance of raid to outside the room
