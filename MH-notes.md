## Trash - Rage Winterchill
- wowhead notes on trash: https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/npc=17767/rage-winterchill#comments;mode:n10
- Ghoul: hits clothies for 3k, enrage: decrease time between attacks by 150% for 8s, cannibalize
- Crypt Fiend: ranged attack for 3x 800, web spit/root
- Abominations: disease cloud - ~800 nature dmg to nearby enemies, knockdown - 2s stun and 4k dmg, hits hard
    - if tank gets chained stun, they will take a lot of damage (cant parry, block, dodge, etc)
- Shadowy Necromancer: most troublesome, fully ranged, shadowbolts for 3k, can be feared/sheeped/ccd
    - unholy frenzy: double friendly unit's attack speed by 100% for 20s, but deals 500 nature dmg every 2s
    - summons 2 constructs (melee or ranged) if a friendly npc dies

Waves:
1) 10 ghouls
2) 10 ghouls 2 crypt fiends
3) 6 ghouls 6 crypt fiends
4) 6 ghouls 4 crypt fiends 2 necromancers
5) 2 ghouls 6 crypt fiends 4 necromancers
6) 6 ghouls 6 abominations(!)
7) 4 ghouls 4 necromancers 4 abominations(!)
8) 6 ghouls 4 crypt fiends 2 abominations 2 necromancers
9) Rage Winterchill

## Rage Winterchill
- ice bolt: player takes 4.2k - 5.7k instant frost dmg; 4 second stun & DoT of 2.5k per second for 4 seconds (10k total)
    - ice bolt will 1-shot players, 
    - medallion of the alliance or living action potion
    - mages can counter w/ blink or ice block, BM hunters can bestial wrath, pallies can bubble
- death and decay: 20 yard AoE, lasts 15 secs, players take 15% of Max HP loss per second as shadow dmg
    - if casted on tank, boss will need to be re-positioned

- frost nova: 20 yard range from boss, 10 second immobilize, one-time 2.7k damage
    - dispell
- frost armor: melee get chilled debuff upon hit; -25% attack speed, -50% movement speed
    - free action potion
    - will hinder tank's threat generation -> free action potion and pally's blessing of freedom

- a combo of DnD and Frost Nova is deadly, esp. on tank
- boss should be tanked near jaina
- [ ] 10 minute beserk timer?
- [ ] is major frost protection potion viable?

- strat:
    - prep: medallion of the alliance, living action potion
    - pull boss to jaina ; hunters can MD boss to tank
    - BL as soon as tank establishes threat
    - range spread out at max range so minimal players are affected by DnD
    - PvP trinket out of frost bolt; dispell frost novas; move out of DnD

Waves:

3) 4 shadowy necromancers, 4 crypt fiends, and 4 ghouls
4) 2 banshees, 6 crypt fiends, and 4 ghouls -Banshees are introduced

## Anetheron
- carrion swarm: targets random player, cone effect AoE, 4.2k shadow damage, reduces healing done by 75% for 20sec
    - casted every 15 secs
    - healers spread out
    - rogue's wound poison can mitigate some healing (reduces healing effects by 10% for 15 seconds, up to 5 stacks)
        - warrior's mortal wound, hunter's aimed shot
- vampiric aura: boss's melee attacks heals him for 3x damage; infernals within 25 yards also get this buff
    - tank infernals away from boss
- inferno: random target, summons an infernal, 2 second stun; happens about once per minute
    - infernal has immolation aura, 3-4k fire dmg every 2 seconds ; and benefits from vampiric aura
    - inferno is a 3 second cast, players targetted by it can run towards OT
- sleep: periodically puts 3 players to sleep, can't be dispelled or broken out of
    - infernal's immolation or carrion swarm can break this out

- putting on some SR gear can be helpful w/ carrion swarm, esp. with healers not getting healing debuff if swarm is resisted
- 10 minutes enrage
- elixir of demon slaying
- care when dps swaps to infernal, as them being bunched up means bad news if they get hit by carrion swam
    - however, both dungeon guides recommend ignoring the infernals (which makes OT fire-res gear necessary)
- immolation and carrion swarm together can be doozy

- strat:
    - prep: off-tank fire res gear; ideally prot pally (~200 unbuffed), pallies have ranged taunt
    - 1 tank for boss, 1 OT for infernals
    - healers spread to avoid too many getting hit from carrion swarm
        - off heals may be helpful
    - OT picks up infernals, tank infernals away from boss;  can possibly be misdirected
        - the healer assigned to OT can be 70 yards away from boss to not be targetted by swam
        - dps ignores infernals
        - move away from infernals when they land to avoid immolation aura
    - wound poison, mortal strike, aimed shot on boss to mitigate healing
    - BL once tank establishes threat

## Trash - Kaz'rogal
- https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/npc=17888/kazrogal#comments
- troll casters drop a healing totem, heals 400 per tick, effects from >1 totem stacks
- ghouls, crypt fiends, abominations, necromancers, banshees
- gargoyles: airborne, need to be aggro'd to ground by ranged; spams a 2-second spell for 2k, melees cloth for 3k
    - sometimes, gargoyles spawn from the back, and can kill npcs, ranged dps should pull them to the front before they get hooked on npcs
- frost wyrms: AoE bolt at their target, 2k; do not land at all, so only ranged can attack them
    - only 1 appears at a time

Waves:
1) 4 abominations, 2 banshees, 4 ghouls, 2 shadowy necromancers
2) 4 ghouls, 10 gargoyles (gargoyles are introduced, run back to get them)
3) 6 crypt fiends, 4 shadowy necromancers, 4 ghouls
4) 6 gargoyles, 6 crypt fiends, 2 shadowy necromancers (run back to get gargoyles)
5) 4 ghouls, 4 shadowy necromancers, 6 abominations (!)
6) 8 gargoyles, 1 frost wyrm (exception, all the mobs enter from the front)
7) Same as 6
8) 2 crypt fiends, 6 ghouls, 2 banshees, 2 shadowy necromancers, 6 abominations (!)
9) Kaz'rogal

## Kaz'rogal
- mark of kaz'rogal: drains 3k mana over 5 seconds (or 600/sec), for every mana user
    - if player has no mana -> ~11k dmg to self and others within 15 yards
    - will be casted more frequently as fight goes on
        - first cast at 45 seconds into the fight, then 40, then 35, etc
    - ret pallies and enh. shamans need to be esp. careful so they dont explode on melee
    - resto druids can shift into cat/bear will make them immune to mana drain
    - this can be resisted periodically using the shadow resist neck; looks like shadow resist gear reduces explosion dmg
- war stomp: 5 second stun within 15 yards, 2k dmg
    - free action potion
- cripple: slows attack and movement speed; reduces strength; casted on random players, pets, npcs
    - cloak of shadows, blessing of freedom; otherwise not dispellable
- malevolent cleave: frontal cleave, damage is split evenly among players
    - position thrall so he's behind the boss, so he doesnt get hit by cleave
    - non-tanks never in front

- pally tank not recommended due to the mana drain mechanic
- elixir of demon slaying
- healer should prio mana-efficient spells
- new entrance to MH unlocks at horde camp

- strat:
    - need to burn boss quickly, move boss around camp to aggro the taurens and thrall; hunters can MD boss to tank
    - mana pots! demonic/dark runes; mana conservation in general; judge wis on boss, mana tide totem, shadow priest
        - mages can negate via ice block, pallies via divine shield
    - run away from others ahead of mark if your mana is about to be completely drained to minimize dmg
    - BL once tank gets threat

## Azgalor

- Doom: targets random player in raid, kills them in 20 seconds and spawns a lesser doomguard; every 50 seconds
    - the doomguard casts cripple: slows attack and movement speed 50%, reduces strength 50%, 12 seconds
        - not dispellable; can be cloak of shadow'd, blessing of freedom
    - doomguard casts war stomp: 5 second stun, 1k dmg
- rain of fire: targets random player within 30 yards and casts RoF, 15 yard AoE that lasts 10 seconds
    - players in RoF get 1.6k fire dmg every 2 seconds
    - players also get Unquenchable Flames debuff, 1.2k fire damage per second for 5 seconds
- Howl of Azgalor: raid-wide silence for 5 seconds; its a shadow effect, so shadow resist gear can resist it
    - cooldown is ~20 seconds?
- Cleave: deals damage to melee in front

- demon slaying elixir
- consider have tank not attack boss while silenced to avoid being parry hasted
    - boss is immune to disarm
- warlocks can cast soulstone on players that have doom
- consider fire res gear for RoF, esp. for melee
- silence can be resisted with shadow res gear, even just medallion of karabor
- alternative strat for doomguard, set up a group of 5 to kill the doomguard (1 tank, 1 healer, 3 dps)
    - doesnt seem ideal; doomguards despawn when boss dies

- Strat:
    - tanked near thrall
    - HoTs to deal with silences
    - priests can buff people with shadow resist; melee consider fire res gear
    - paladins w/ improved concentration aura can be grouped w/ healers to reduce silence (-30% duration; 5->3.5secs)
    - for RoF, ranged+healer can stand >30 yards, so RoF is only casted on melee; melee+tank will have to move
        - or just have ranged spread out around boss so minimal players get hit with RoF; different sources cite different strats
    - doomguard: doomed player goes to offtank (by tauren warriors)
        - doomguards are ignored by dps, tanked by offtank until boss dies
        - free action potions can help if tank is stunned via warstomp, but needs to pick up another doomguard

## Archimonde

- Doomfire: spawns near archimonde, follows a random player, leaving behind a trail of fire, 2.4k damage per second
    - also a DoT that deals decreasing damage over time
    - lasts for about 45 seconds, common to have 2 on ground
    - DoT can be cloak of shadow'd, divine shield, ice block
- Air Burst: targets a random player, that player and other players within 13 yards get thrown into the air + 3k nature dmg
    - Tear of the goddess to prevent fall damage; has short duration, so gotta activate it near the end of the fall
- Fear: raid-wide fear for 8 secs; about every 40 seconds
    - fear ward, tremor totem, medallion of alliance, warrior with beserker rage
- Grip of the Legion: targets random player, 5 minutes, 2.5k shadow damage every 2 seconds; dispellable by mages, druids
- Finger of Death: only casted of there are no melee near him; 20k shadow dmg
- Soul Charge: raid-wide effect that gets triggered by a player death
    - priest, mage, warlock: 4.5k fire damage, 4s silence
    - warrior, rogue, paladin: 4.5k phys damage, increases dmg taken by 50% for 8s
    - druid, shaman, hunter: 4.5k nature dmg over 8s, drains 2.2k mana

- speak to tyrande before fight to get tear of the goddess
- fear + doomfire/finger of death can be deadly
    - ensure tank is never feared to avoid FoD
- at boss 10% health, fight is effectively over, tyrande casts protection of elune on all raiders, making them immune to dmg
- only 1 tank is needed, others heal/damage; warrior tank is preferred
    - boss hits like a truck, but no crushing blows, can parry haste
- ideally 1 shaman and 1 dispeller per group, groups stay together
- pvp trinket is pretty important 
- if we struggle with mechanics here, might be worth doing practice runs where people don't dps and just focus on mechanics; try to survive for 5 minutes

- Strat:
    - avoid all deaths; stay away from doomfires
    - dispel Grip of the Legion
    - shaman tremor totem for fears
    - loosely stacked for air burst
    - medallion of alliance if tremor totem doesnt work
